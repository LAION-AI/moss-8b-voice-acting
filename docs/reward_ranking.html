What am I looking at?

The same 98 voice-clone prompts as the genuineness grid (same reference speaker, same performance instruction, same paraphrased text), each sampled 10 times at temperature 1.0 from the fine-tuned MOSS-TTS 8B model. Here the 10 takes are ranked not by genuineness alone but by a combined reward that folds in perceptual quality (VoiceCLAP-small blend head, 0–10) and transcription fidelity (invWER from Parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3) alongside genuineness.

rewardP = invWER × ( norm(blend) + norm(genuP) )
invWER = max(0, 1 − WER)  ∈ [0,1]  (gates the whole reward → garbled takes score 0)
norm(·) = global min–max to [0,1] across all 962 candidates (blend, genu each normalized separately)
P ∈ { VoiceCLAP-large-v2 (3584-d), VoiceCLAP-commercial (768-d) } — shown as two separate columns

Min–max (not z-score) keeps the reward non-negative and monotone; the two genuineness terms sum with blend to [0,2], then invWER scales to [0,1]×…. Within every group we rank the 10 takes descending by reward — separately for each predictor — and show the Top-3 (green) and Bottom-2 (amber). Under each take: its combined reward and the three components (blend 0–10, genu, invWER/WER). The group's original reference clip and the spoken paraphrase are shown for context.

Reproduce study

Reproduce-and-improve, ranked two ways
25 expressive reference performances × 3 temperatures × best-of-8. Clone each voice, then rank the takes with two independent rewards: a voice-identity combined reward (ECAPA speaker-sim + invWER + genuineness + blend + duration) and an emotion-profile reward — cosine of a 42-dim EmoNet emotion vector (Empathic-Insight-Voice-Plus on BUD-E-Whisper) × invWER. Per temperature × group: reference clip + prompt + best-of-8 top-3 under both rankings side by side. They disagree on ~48% of takes.
